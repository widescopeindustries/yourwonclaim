info What is VA Math?

The VA doesn't just add ratings. They subtract from your "non-disabled" body.

Example: A 50% rating leaves you 50% efficient. A second 50% rating is 50% of your remaining efficiency (25%), giving you a combined 75%, which rounds to 80%.

How the 2025 VA Disability Calculator Works

Calculating your VA disability rating can feel like a nightmare. The Department of Veterans Affairs uses a specialized "combined rating table" that isn't as simple as adding 50% + 50%. This is often referred to as "VA Math."

Our calculator uses the official 2025 VA disability pay rates, which include the 2.5% Cost of Living Adjustment (COLA). These new rates were finalized in late 2024 and apply to all checks received starting in January 2025.

What is the Bilateral Factor?

If you have disabilities affecting both sides of your body (e.g., both knees, both shoulders), the VA adds a "Bilateral Factor." This adds an additional 10% to the combined value of the bilateral disabilities before they are added to your non-bilateral ratings. This can often be the difference between rounding from an 84% to an 85% (which becomes a 90% rating).
