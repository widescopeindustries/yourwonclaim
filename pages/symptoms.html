Recognizing the Impact

Not Your Grandfather's
"Shell Shock"

Modern PTSD includes symptoms many veterans don’t recognize as service-connected. Understanding these is the first step toward your 100% P&T.

visibility

Hypervigilance

Constant scanning, back-to-wall seating, exit-checking in public places.

groups_off

Social Avoidance

Skipping public events, isolating at home, avoiding crowds or restaurants.

heart_broken

Relationship Strain

Persistent anger, emotional distance, and difficulty connecting with family.

bedtime

Sleep Disruption

Vivid nightmares, chronic insomnia, or hyperarousal during the night.

warning

Startle Response

Exaggerated physical reactions to sudden sounds, movements, or surprises.

bolt

Irritability

Quick temper, persistent tension, and poor tolerance for everyday frustrations.

Sound Familiar?

You're not alone. These are recognized service-connected symptoms. The strategic path to your rating starts here.
