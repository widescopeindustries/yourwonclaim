The VA Isn't Judging Your Pain.
It's Scoring Your Language.

VA raters don't evaluate how bad your condition feels. They evaluate how well your evidence matches the diagnostic criteria for your specific condition code. It's a vocabulary — and it's one most veterans were never taught.

That's not a flaw in the veteran. It's a gap in the system. The VA never hands you a glossary. Nobody explains that "functional impairment" and "frequency of flare-ups" are the specific things being scored. You're just expected to figure it out — or hire someone who already knows.

These templates close that gap. Every condition has a code. Every code has criteria. These templates teach you both — so you stop trying to navigate an entire bureaucracy and start knowing exactly what it says about your story.
