The Mindset That Wins

“I am here to get the maximum amount of benefits available to me — 100% P&T if earned — for conditions caused by my military service.”

Lock the Target. Then Align Everything Else.

When the target is clear, decisions get simpler. You’re not “hoping for a rating.” You’re proving — honestly and thoroughly — the true impact of your service‑connected conditions, aiming at the maximum outcome you’ve earned.

Three Pillars

  • Clarity: Know the target (100% P&T if earned). Avoid scattered claims.
  • Resolve: You will be persistent, patient, and truthful at every step.
  • Integrity: Document real symptoms, real limits, real examples — nothing exaggerated.

Reset After Denials

Denials don’t mean you’re wrong — often they mean the claim wasn’t framed or evidenced correctly. Reset your mindset: learn, adjust, and move forward with a focused plan.

Peeks at the Strategy (Not the Whole Playbook)

  • Lead with claims that have lower proof burden and higher impact.
  • Modern symptoms matter more than old labels — describe today’s reality.
  • Prepare for C&P with specific, truthful examples of functional limits.
