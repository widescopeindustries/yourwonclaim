Questions

Frequently Asked

Is this official VA documentation? +
No. This is supplemental evidence you create yourself to support your claim. The VA allows and considers personal statements and logs as evidence. Our templates are designed to capture the exact criteria raters evaluate.
Will this guarantee a higher rating? +
Nothing can guarantee a specific rating—that depends on your actual condition and medical evidence. What these templates do is ensure you're documenting your symptoms in the format and language that raters are trained to evaluate.
I already have my C&P exam scheduled. Is it too late? +
Ideally, you'd have 30 days of documentation. But even 1-2 weeks of detailed logging is better than nothing. Start today. You can also continue logging and submit additional evidence after your exam.
What format are the templates? +
PDF format. You can print them directly, fill them out digitally on a tablet, or use the format as a guide for your own documentation. Print as many copies as you need.
