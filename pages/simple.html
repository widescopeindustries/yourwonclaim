Here's What Nobody Tells You
About VA Claims

VA raters don't evaluate how bad your condition feels. They evaluate how well your evidence matches the diagnostic criteria for your specific condition code. It's a language — and most vets don't know it.

That's not a knock on veterans. The VA system was never designed to be intuitive. But once you understand that your job is to document in the language the rater is scoring against, the whole process gets a lot clearer.

That's what these templates do. Every field, every prompt, every section maps to what a rater is actually looking for when they open your file. Stop guessing. Start documenting in the right language.
