The Legal Grounding

Most veterans assume they don't qualify because they weren't "in combat." This is a critical misconception.

gavel 38 CFR § 3.304(f) and VA Manual M21-1

Combat-related PTSD includes:

Direct Combat Exposure

Combat Training (Exercises/Live-fire)

Combat Support Activities

Combat Zone Service (Any)

error NO SPECIFIC STRESSOR PROOF

If your PTSD is determined to be combat-related, the VA cannot require you to provide evidence of specific stressors. This is the game-changer most veterans miss.
