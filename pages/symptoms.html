This Isn’t Your Grandfather’s “Shell Shock”

Modern PTSD includes symptoms many veterans don’t recognize as service‑connected.

Hypervigilance

Constant scanning, back‑to‑wall seating, exit‑checking.

Social Avoidance

Skipping public places, isolating, avoiding crowds.

Relationship Strain

Anger, emotional distance, difficulty connecting.

Sleep Disruption

Nightmares, insomnia, hyperarousal at night.

Startle Response

Exaggerated reactions to sudden sounds or movements.

Irritability

Quick temper, persistent tension, poor frustration tolerance.

Sound Familiar?

These are service‑connected PTSD symptoms. The strategic path starts here.
